## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am **Dr. Usama Arshad**, an AI and blockchain researcher, educator, and software developer working at the intersection of **intelligent systems, cybersecurity, digital forensics, and practical software engineering**.

My work spans academic research, university teaching, student mentorship, and the development of usable applications. I am particularly interested in translating research concepts into secure, scalable tools that solve real-world problems.

- 🎓 **Academic experience:** Assistant Professor in Business Analytics at FAST National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES); previously Graduate Teaching Assistant at GIKI.
- 🔬 **Research interests:** Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Blockchain, Digital Forensics, Cybersecurity, Quantum Machine Learning, and trustworthy AI.
- 🧪 **Research and development:** CRADLE Lab; interdisciplinary AI and secure-systems projects.
- 👨‍🏫 **Teaching and mentorship:** AI, deep learning, blockchain, digital forensics, cybersecurity, Python, and business data/text mining.
- 🛠️ **Applied development:** Full-stack platforms, analytics dashboards, blockchain applications, educational tools, and research prototypes.
- 🌍 **Open to:** Research collaborations, visiting research opportunities, postdoctoral research, industry partnerships, and impactful open-source projects.

> **My focus:** Build systems that are intelligent, secure, explainable, and useful beyond the research paper.
